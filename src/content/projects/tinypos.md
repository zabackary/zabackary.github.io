tinyPOS is a "tiny" and simple point-of-sale system developed to keep track of
stock and revenues simply for events like school festivals. I initially
developed it in Japanese for a friend but later translated it to English for use
at my own school.

Because internet connectivity is often unstable outside, it has full offline
support with several redundant backends after it is installed as a PWA.
